\newcommand\ipaipsum{
    The International Phonetic Alphabet [ˌɪntəˈnæʃənəl fəˈnɛtɪk ˈælfəbɛt] was developed in the 19th century.
    Most symbols are based on the Latin script, with modifications. For example: [ʃ ʈ ɖ ɟ ɽ ɻ ʂ ʐ ʙ ⱱ ɬ ħ ɦ]. Other symbols are based on the Greek script: [ɸ β θ ð ɣ χ]. Diacritics are placed above, below or on either side of main symbols: [ɵ̈ ɞ̽ œ̃], [m̥ d̥ ʐ̥ ɵ̞ ɞ̘ œ̤], [pʰ ʐˠ tʷ]. Three diacritics have special placement: [p̚ ʐ̴ ɨ˞].
    Multiple diacritics should get stacked: [i̋ ɯ̟̞ e̞̽ æ̃ ɑ̰ ɒ̈ ɶ̙ ʉ̠ ɵ̞ ɤ̝ ɞ̟ ə̠ ɜ̽ ɚ̤ ɐ̠ a̋ a̰].
}
